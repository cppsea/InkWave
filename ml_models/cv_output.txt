   What is Machine Learning?
   Definition: A branch of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn patterns from
   data and make decisions or predictions without explicit programming
   Goal: Build models that generalize well to unseen data.
   Types of Machine Learning
   1. Supervised Learning
   O
   Training Data: Labeled data (input-output pairs)
   O
   Goal: Learn a mapping from inputs to outputs
   Examples:
   Regression (e.g., predicting house prices).
   Classification (e.g., spam detection).
   2. Unsupervised Learning.
   Training Data: Unlabeled data.
   O
   Goal: Find hidden patterns or structure in data.
   O
   Examples:
   Clustering (e.g., customer segmentation)
   Dimensionality reduction (e.g., PCA)
