<b>Machine Learning</b>

<br><b>Definition:</b> A <span style="background-color: yellow">branch</span> of <span style="background-color: yellow">artificial intelligence</span> that <span style="background-color: yellow">enables systems</span> to <span style="background-color: yellow">learn patterns</span> from <span style="background-color: yellow">data</span> and <span style="background-color: yellow">make decisions</span> or <span style="background-color: yellow">predictions without explicit programming</span>.

<br><b>Goal:</b> Build models that <span style="background-color: yellow">generalize well</span> to <span style="background-color: yellow">unseen data</span>.

<br><b>Types of Machine Learning:</b>

<br><span style="background-color: yellow">1</span>. **Supervised Learning**
<br>   - **Training Data:** Labeled data (<span style="background-color: yellow">input</span>-<span style="background-color: yellow">output pairs</span>)
<br>   - **Goal:** Learn a <span style="background-color: yellow">mapping</span> from <span style="background-color: yellow">inputs</span> to <span style="background-color: yellow">outputs</span>
<br>   - **Examples:**
<br>     - <span style="background-color: yellow">Regression</span> (<span style="background-color: yellow">e</span>.g., predicting house prices)
<br>     - <span style="background-color: yellow">Classification</span> (<span style="background-color: yellow">e</span>.g., spam detection)
<br>
<br><span style="background-color: yellow">2</span>. **Unsupervised Learning**
<br>   - **Training Data:** Unlabeled data
<br>   - **Goal:** Find hidden patterns or <span style="background-color: yellow">structure</span> in <span style="background-color: yellow">data</span>
<br>   - **Examples:**
<br>     - <span style="background-color: yellow">Clustering</span> (<span style="background-color: yellow">e</span>.g., customer segmentation)
<br>     - <span style="background-color: yellow">Dimensionality reduction</span> (<span style="background-color: yellow">e</span>.g., PCA)<br>
